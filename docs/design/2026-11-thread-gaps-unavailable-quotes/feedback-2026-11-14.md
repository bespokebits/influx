# Result: missing posts in threads and unavailable quotes

The round closed on 2026-11-14.

- **Missing posts in a thread: column `C`, a washed note**, on both
  `ios-thread-gaps` and `mac-thread-gaps`. A note on the wash colour sits
  at the edge of its list: above the first earlier post for gaps among the
  earlier posts, below the last later post for gaps after it. It has a
  heading ("EARLIER POSTS MISSING" or "LATER POSTS MISSING"), one sentence
  per reason, and "Read the whole thread on <site>", which opens the post's
  own page. It says "some posts", never a count.
- **A quote of an unavailable post: column `B`, a hairline frame**, on
  `ios-row-unavailable-quote`, `mac-row-unavailable-quote`,
  `ios-thread-unavailable-quote` and `mac-thread-unavailable-quote`. The
  quote card's hairline box holds an italic "Quoted post unavailable" line
  and the site, and opens the quoted post's address. In the timeline its
  height is fixed: the card's padding, one line at the quote's text size
  and one meta line, every line cut short.
- **How it was decided:** the owner took the coordinator's recommendation
  for `C` and `B`. Nobody voted or left a note on the review site, so there
  are no votes, notes or preferences to record here.
- **Why this mix:** a gap changes how the whole thread reads, so it earns
  the heavier note and a way to the complete conversation. An unavailable
  quote stands where a quote card would be, so it keeps the card's frame
  and stays quiet.
